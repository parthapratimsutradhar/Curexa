About Doctor

Professional Bio

Dr. Sarah Chen is a highly skilled cardiologist with over 15 years of experience in the field of cardiovascular medicine. She completed her residency at Johns Hopkins Hospital and fellowship in interventional cardiology at Cleveland Clinic. Dr. Chen is passionate about providing personalized care to her patients and believes in a holistic approach to heart health.

She has published numerous research papers in prestigious medical journals and regularly participates in international cardiology conferences. Her areas of expertise include coronary artery disease, heart valve disorders, arrhythmias, and preventive cardiology.
