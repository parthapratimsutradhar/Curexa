What is Dolo 650 used for?

Dolo 650 is a pain reliever and fever reducer. It effectively treats headaches, body aches, fever, dental pain, and pain from cold/flu.

Key Benefits

  • Fast relief from fever
  • Effective pain relief
  • Minimal stomach irritation
  • Safe for most adults when used as directed

Important Notes

  • Do not exceed 4 tablets (2600mg) in 24 hours
  • Consult doctor if fever persists for more than 3 days
  • Avoid alcohol while taking this medicine
  • Not recommended for children under 12 without doctor's advice

Dosage Instructions

Recommended Dose

  • Adults: 1 tablet every 4-6 hours
  • Children (12+): As directed by doctor
  • Max per day: 4 tablets (2600mg)
  • Duration: As needed, not more than 10 days without consultation

How to Take

  • Swallow whole with water
  • Can be taken with or without food
  • If stomach upset occurs, take with food
  • Avoid alcohol completely

Do not exceed the recommended dose. Overdose can cause serious liver damage. Stop use and consult doctor if you experience yellowing of skin/eyes, dark urine, or severe nausea.
